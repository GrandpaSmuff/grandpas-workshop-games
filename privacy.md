## Summary in one paragraph

Realm & Rune is a single-player iOS card game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers — in fact, we do not operate any servers. All of your game progress is stored locally on your device. In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple, and we never see your payment details. The only network connection the app makes is to Google's font CDN to load the typefaces used by the game.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect personal information.** Specifically:

- We do not require an account or login.
- We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact information.
- We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone audio, camera images, health data, or any other sensitive information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not maintain any server that receives data from your device.

## Information stored on your device

Your game progress (decks, gems, unlocks, settings) is stored locally on your device using iOS's standard storage (`UserDefaults`). This information stays on your device. It is included in your iCloud Device Backup if you have iCloud Backup enabled — that backup is managed by Apple, not by us.

When you delete the app, this local data is removed.

## In-app purchases

Realm & Rune offers optional in-app purchases (gem packs and content unlocks). All in-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. **We do not receive your name, credit card number, or any other payment details.** We only receive a confirmation from Apple that a transaction was completed, identifying the product purchased.

Apple's handling of your payment is governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

For refund requests, contact Apple at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

## Third-party services

The app loads its fonts from Google Fonts at `fonts.googleapis.com` and `fonts.gstatic.com`. When fonts are requested, your device's IP address is transmitted to Google. Google's handling of this information is governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive or store this information.

We do not use any other third-party services, analytics, advertising networks, or tracking technologies.

## Children's privacy

Realm & Rune is **not directed to children under 13**. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided personal information through the app, please contact us — though, as stated above, we do not collect personal information from anyone.

The App Store age rating for Realm & Rune is 9+, reflecting stylized fantasy combat. The age rating is independent of the policy above.

## Your rights

Even though we do not collect personal information, you have certain rights under applicable privacy laws:

### California (CCPA/CPRA)

- We do not sell or share personal information.
- We do not collect personal information about California residents (or anyone else) to delete, port, or correct.
- You have the right to non-discrimination for exercising any privacy right.

### European Union / EEA / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

At launch, Realm & Rune is not available in EU/EEA/UK App Stores. If we expand to those regions in the future, we will provide additional disclosures here, and users in those regions will have the rights afforded by GDPR (access, deletion, correction, portability, restriction of processing, objection, and withdrawal of consent). Since we collect no personal data, exercising these rights is largely moot — but the rights still exist.

### Other jurisdictions

Rights vary by location. Contact us with any specific question.
